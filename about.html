About Mutovu TSS

Discover our identity, purpose and commitment to education.

Welcome to Mutovu TSS

Mutovu Technical Secondary School is dedicated to technical education, practical learning and the development of knowledge and professional skills.


Our website provides information about our school, technical departments, learning tutorials, school activities, announcements and admissions.


We believe education should prepare learners to contribute positively to their communities and the development of society.

Our Vision, Mission and Values

The principles that guide our educational journey.

Our Vision

To become a recognized institution of technical education that promotes skills, innovation and excellence.

Our Mission

To support quality learning and practical skills development that prepares learners for employment and further education.

Our Values

Discipline, respect, teamwork, creativity, responsibility, integrity and excellence.

Our Commitment to Technical Education

Technical education gives learners opportunities to develop practical abilities and solve real-world problems.


Through learning, creativity and practical activities, students can develop the confidence and skills needed for their future careers.
