Three Trades, Many Opportunities

Explore our three technical fields of study.

Building Construction

Learn construction techniques, building materials, measurements, site preparation and practical building skills.


Skills Developed

• Construction practices
• Technical drawing
• Measurement and estimation
• Building materials
• Site safety


Career Opportunities

Construction technician, site assistant, building estimator and further technical study.

Software Development

Explore programming, website creation, databases, application development and computer problem-solving.


Skills Developed

• HTML and CSS
• JavaScript programming
• Database management
• Web development
• Software testing


Career Opportunities

Junior web developer, software developer, database assistant and IT technician.

Multimedia

Develop creative digital skills through photography, graphic design, audio, video production and digital media.


Skills Developed

• Photography
• Graphic design
• Video editing
• Audio production
• Digital content creation


Career Opportunities

Graphic designer, photographer, video editor, multimedia creator and digital content producer.

Learning Through Practice

Technical education combines theoretical knowledge with practical activities. Learners develop skills by working on projects, solving problems and using relevant tools.


At Mutovu TSS, our three trades represent different opportunities for creativity, innovation and professional development.
